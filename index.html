How long does a website take?
Timing depends on the number of pages, features and how quickly the required text, photos and feedback are provided. We'll agree on a realistic timeline after discussing your project rather than promise a fixed turnaround for every website.
Who owns the website and domain?
The intention is for you to own your website files and have the access needed to manage your site. If you buy a custom domain, register it in your own name and pay the registrar directly. Hosting and any third-party services have their own terms and fees.
Do I need to know technical things?
No coding knowledge is needed to discuss your project. Share your business details and goals, and we'll explain the steps in plain language. Some hosting or domain setup may still require you to approve actions in your own account.
Can I update the website later?
Yes. For this static website approach, text and images can be changed in the HTML and image files. We'll provide a simple guide at handover. If you want a visual editor or content management system, tell us before the project is scoped because that changes the setup and cost.
How do payments work?
The planned payment arrangement is 50% upfront to begin work and 50% before final handover. The project scope and final price should be agreed in writing before the first payment. Domain fees are paid by you directly to the registrar.
